About

Roman Rusinov is a designer, illustrator and maker. He specializes in product design, from idea to development (or manufacturing), developing brand identity, bringing it to market, promotion strategies, website design and development.

He has experience working with software-as-a-service companies, where he designed mobile experiences, user interfaces and interactions; designed and developed marketing websites; and drew hundreds of icons. He also does commercial illustration, art, print, video and photo production — and self-initiates projects from time to time.

Away from the desk, Roman is an avid mountain biker, fisherman and great-outdoors advocate.
